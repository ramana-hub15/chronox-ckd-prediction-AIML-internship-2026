Privacy Policy & Medical Disclaimer

1. Educational & Screening Support Disclaimer

ChronoX is designed exclusively for educational, research, and preliminary screening support. Risk percentages, eGFR estimates, and stage hints generated by the machine learning models do not constitute medical advice or diagnosis. Never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice based on results from this application.

2. Data Storage & User Control

Screening entries submitted while signed in are stored locally in the application SQLite database solely so you can review history, track trends, and export PDF/CSV reports. You may delete any individual screening record at any time from the History page.

3. Security Practices

Passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashes (Werkzeug), forms are protected by CSRF tokens, and login routes enforce rate limiting.
